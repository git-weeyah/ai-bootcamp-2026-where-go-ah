# ICA - Loss of Identity Card

AGENCY: Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA)
CATEGORY: Identity
TOPIC: Lost Singapore Identity Card
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/ic/loss
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## Key information
If a Singapore Identity Card (IC) is lost, the loss should be reported to ICA immediately. A replacement application must be made within 14 days from the report.

A police report is not required for loss and replacement of the IC. The loss must still be reported to ICA through its e-Service.

If the lost IC is found before a replacement application is submitted, the person should contact ICA through the enquiry form for assistance.

Once a replacement application has been submitted, a new IC will be issued and the fee is non-refundable.

## Fees
- First loss: S$100
- Second and subsequent losses: S$300

## Processing
ICA states that applications are processed within seven working days, although this can take longer during peak periods or if photographs/supporting documents do not meet requirements.

After approval, the applicant will be notified by SMS and email and should make an appointment to collect the IC within three months.

## RAG response rule
For "I lost my IC lah, how?" route to ICA. Do not tell the user that a police report is required for an IC loss unless current ICA information specifically says so.
