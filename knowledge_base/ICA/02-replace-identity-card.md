# ICA - Replace Identity Card

AGENCY: Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA)
CATEGORY: Identity
TOPIC: Replace Identity Card - Change of Particulars or Damaged IC
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/ic/update_particulars
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## Key information
IC holders may apply for a replacement when they need to change particulars or photograph.

If an IC is damaged or defaced, the holder must apply for a replacement within 14 days from the date of damage or defacement.

Applications can be made online through ICA's e-Service using Singpass.

## Fee
The replacement fee stated on the ICA page is S$60.

## Processing
ICA states that applications are processed within seven working days, although this may take longer during peak periods or when photographs/supporting documents do not meet requirements.

After approval, the applicant will be notified by SMS and email and should make an appointment to collect the IC within three months.

## RAG response rule
If the user says "IC spoil already", "my IC damaged", or wants to change their IC particulars, retrieve this document. If the user says the IC is lost, retrieve the Loss of Identity Card document instead.
