## Purpose
Use this document for citizen questions about Singapore passports, including passport application, renewal, collection, and related requirements.

## RAG routing
If a user asks "How to renew my passport?", "passport expiring lah", or "where apply Singapore passport?", route the question to ICA's passport services.

## Who can apply or renew online
First-time applicants, or those whose passport is running out of pages, has expired or expires within nine months, or is lost or damaged.

## Fees
- Online: S$70
- Over the counter (ICA Services Centre or Singapore Overseas Mission): S$80
- Lost or damaged passport: passport fee plus a non-refundable penalty of S$50 for the first replacement and S$100 for each subsequent one
- First passport fee is waived for newborn citizens if applied via MyICA on or before the child's first birthday

## Processing
About 1 to 2 weeks in Singapore (up to 4 weeks if the photo is non-compliant or the principal name or surname changes); about 4 to 6 weeks overseas. The outcome is sent by email.

## Validity
10 years for citizens aged 16 and above; 5 years for citizens under 16.

## Steps
Prepare a digital colour passport-sized photo taken within the last three months, log in to the ICA e-Service with Singpass, complete the form and pay online.
