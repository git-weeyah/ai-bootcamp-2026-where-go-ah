## Flat types
The Public Rental Scheme provides different flat types depending on household size and the applicable scheme.

Examples from HDB's current information include:
- Family Scheme: a 2-person household may be offered a 1-room flat.
- Family Scheme: a household of more than 2 persons may be offered a 2-room flat.
- Joint Singles Scheme: a 2-person household may be offered a 1-room flat, including partitioned flats.

## Locations
HDB provides information on rental flat locations and vacant flats. The list of vacant flats available to approved applicants is sent to those applicants.

## RAG response rule
If a user asks "Where are HDB rental flats located?", explain that HDB provides the current locations and availability information on its official page. Do not treat the sample location list as a permanent inventory because vacancies can change.
