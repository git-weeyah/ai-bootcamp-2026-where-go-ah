## Purpose
This document helps identify the HDB rental options available to someone who wants to rent a flat in Singapore.

## Key information
HDB provides several rental options, including:
- Public Rental Scheme
- Parenthood Provisional Housing Scheme
- Renting from the open market

The Public Rental Scheme is for eligible households who have no family support or other housing options. Eligibility is assessed based on the applicant's circumstances.

Renting from the open market is different from applying for a subsidised public rental flat. A tenant rents an HDB flat or bedroom from an existing flat owner, subject to HDB rules.

## Routing guidance
If a user says they want to "rent an HDB", "rent a government flat", or asks generally where to start with HDB rental, direct them to HDB's Renting a Flat page first. Ask a clarifying question if it is unclear whether they mean the Public Rental Scheme or open-market rental.

## Important
Do not determine a user's eligibility from this document alone. Refer to the specific eligibility page and the user's circumstances.
