# HDB Open Market Rental - Tenant Eligibility

AGENCY: Housing & Development Board (HDB)
CATEGORY: Housing
TOPIC: Renting an HDB Flat or Bedroom from the Open Market
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/renting-from-open-market/eligibility
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## Who can rent
A tenant may be:
- A Singapore Citizen
- A Singapore Permanent Resident
- A non-citizen legally residing in Singapore who holds an Employment Pass, S Pass, Work Permit, Student Pass, Dependant Pass, or Long-Term Social Visit Pass, subject to HDB's requirements

For non-citizen tenants, the relevant pass generally needs to have at least 6 months' validity at the date of application by the flat owner. Additional nationality and sector-specific conditions apply to some Work Permit holders.

Tourists are not allowed to rent an HDB flat or bedroom.

## Existing housing and property ownership
There are restrictions on tenants who are already tenants of a public rental HDB flat or owners of HDB flats, with exceptions under specific circumstances. There are also restrictions involving Executive Condominium ownership before the applicable minimum occupation period is met.

## Protecting the tenant
A prospective tenant should:
- Visit the flat before renting.
- Check documentary proof that the person offering the flat is the rightful owner.
- For whole-flat rental, ask the owner for HDB's confirmation of eligibility to rent out the flat.
- Ensure the owner has HDB's approval to rent out the flat or bedroom.
- Check that the tenant has been registered as an authorised tenant.

## Rental duration
HDB states that tenants must rent an HDB flat or bedroom for at least 6 months.

## RAG response rule
If a user asks "Can foreigners rent HDB?", do not answer with a simple yes/no. Explain that certain legally residing non-citizens can qualify, subject to HDB's requirements, and that tourists cannot rent HDB flats or bedrooms.
