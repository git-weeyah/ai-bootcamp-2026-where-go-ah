# HDB Public Rental Scheme - Application and Selection

AGENCY: Housing & Development Board (HDB)
CATEGORY: Housing
TOPIC: Public Rental Scheme Application
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/public-rental-scheme/application-process/application-and-selection-of-rental-flat
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## How to apply
Eligible applicants can apply to rent a flat under the Public Rental Scheme through HDB's e-Service.

## Documents commonly required

### Family Scheme
Applicants may need:
- NRIC or birth certificate of proposed occupiers
- Income documents for the applicant and proposed occupiers
- Divorce documents or Deed of Separation, where applicable
- Bank statement showing the value of investments using the CPF Ordinary Account

### Joint Singles Scheme
Applicants may need:
- Income documents of the applicant
- Divorce documents or Deed of Separation, where applicable
- Bank statement showing the value of investments using the CPF Ordinary Account
- Proposed co-applicant's full name, mobile number and email address

## RAG response rule
When a user asks "How do I apply for HDB public rental?", explain that an eligible applicant can apply through HDB's e-Service and provide the official HDB application page.

If the user asks what documents are needed, mention that requirements differ by scheme and that the official HDB page should be checked for the latest requirements.
