## Rent
Under the Public Rental Scheme, monthly rent varies according to factors including:
- Monthly household income
- Flat type
- Whether the applicant is a first-timer or second-timer

HDB publishes the applicable rental rates and may revise them. The RAG system should therefore prefer the latest official HDB source when answering questions about exact rental amounts.

## Deposit and first rent
At key collection, the tenant needs to pay:
- A one-month rent deposit
- The first month of rent

HDB states that no payment is required before key collection.

## First-timer and second-timer
HDB distinguishes applicants based on previous housing ownership and housing subsidies. The exact definitions should be retrieved from the current HDB page when answering detailed eligibility or rental-rate questions.

## RAG response rule
Avoid hard-coding rental amounts into an answer if the knowledge base may become outdated. If exact prices are requested, cite the current HDB rents-and-deposits source.
