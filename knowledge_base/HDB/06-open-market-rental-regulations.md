# HDB Open Market Rental - Regulations

LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## Key rules
HDB has regulations governing the renting of HDB flats and bedrooms from the open market.

A tenant must generally rent an HDB flat or bedroom for at least 6 months.

For non-Malaysian non-citizen tenants renting a whole HDB flat, a Non-Citizen Quota applies at neighbourhood and block levels. When the applicable quota is reached, only Singaporeans and Malaysians may rent a flat in that neighbourhood or block. The quota does not apply to the renting out of bedrooms.

Prospective tenants can use HDB's e-Service to check whether an HDB flat can be rented by a non-Malaysian non-citizen.
