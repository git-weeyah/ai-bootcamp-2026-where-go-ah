# HDB Public Rental Scheme - Eligibility

AGENCY: Housing & Development Board (HDB)
CATEGORY: Housing
TOPIC: Public Rental Scheme Eligibility
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/public-rental-scheme/eligibility
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## Purpose
This document provides high-level eligibility information for HDB's Public Rental Scheme (PRS).

## General principle
A person may apply under the Public Rental Scheme as a family or as a single person, depending on the applicable scheme. HDB assesses applications holistically, taking into account factors such as household income, household size, housing budget and individual circumstances.

## Family Scheme
Key conditions include:
- The applicant must be a Singapore Citizen.
- The applicant must be at least 21 years old.
- The applicant must include another Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident to form a family nucleus.
- The recognised family nucleus depends on circumstances, such as spouses, parents, siblings, or children under the applicant's sole care and control.

Certain single-parent situations may also be considered.

## Joint Singles Scheme
Key conditions include:
- Applicants must be Singapore Citizens.
- Applicants generally need to be at least 35 years old if single, divorced or legally separated.
- Widowed or orphaned applicants may qualify from age 21, subject to the applicable conditions.

## Property ownership
Everyone in the application should not have ownership, interest or estate in property, whether in Singapore or overseas. Previous property ownership may be considered when HDB assesses the applicant's housing budget.

## Existing HDB residents
Applicants should not be owners or essential occupiers of an HDB flat. Existing tenants under HDB rental schemes are also generally not eligible.

## Ability to afford other housing
Applicants may not be eligible if HDB assesses that they can afford other housing options, such as renting from the open market or purchasing a flat.

## Family support
Applicants may not be eligible if their children can house them or are financially able to provide housing. HDB assesses family support as part of the application.

## RAG response rule
Do not say "you are definitely eligible" based only on a user's short description. Explain that HDB assesses applications based on eligibility conditions and individual circumstances, and direct the user to the official HDB eligibility information.
