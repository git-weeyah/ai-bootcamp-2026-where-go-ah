## Purpose
Use this document to route broad family-support questions to MSF and related government services.

## Relevant support areas
MSF provides information and services related to children and families. Examples include:
- Baby Bonus
- Child Development Account (CDA)
- Family and parenthood support
- Large Families Scheme
- Marriage Journey, Children & Families, Women Empowerment and Disability Services (sections on the MSF Support Families & Parents page)
- Services available through LifeSG

Note: the SG Child Support Package, announced at the 2026 National Day Rally, is stated by MSF to replace the Baby Bonus and Large Families schemes.

The exact benefit, eligibility, amount, and application route depends on the programme and the family's circumstances.

## RAG response rule
For a broad question such as "I got baby already, what government help can get?", do not guess a single benefit. Retrieve the latest relevant MSF/LifeSG information and present the applicable programmes with their official source.
