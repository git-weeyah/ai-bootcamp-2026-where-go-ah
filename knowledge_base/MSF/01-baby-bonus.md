# MSF - Baby Bonus Scheme

AGENCY: Ministry of Social and Family Development (MSF)
CATEGORY: Family
TOPIC: Baby Bonus
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.msf.gov.sg/what-we-do/support-families-and-parents/article/programmes/Baby-Bonus-Scheme
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## Key information
The Baby Bonus Scheme supports parents with the cost of raising children.

The scheme includes:
- Baby Bonus Cash Gift
- Child Development Account (CDA) benefits

## Benefits per child (Singapore Citizen children born on or after 18 Feb 2025)
| Birth order | Baby Bonus Cash Gift | CDA First Step Grant | Max government co-matching |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st child | $11,000 | $5,000 | $4,000 |
| 2nd child | $11,000 | $5,000 | $7,000 |
| 3rd child | $13,000 | $10,000 | $9,000 |
| 4th child | $13,000 | $10,000 | $9,000 |
| 5th and subsequent | $13,000 | $10,000 | $15,000 |

The First Step Grant is a CDA deposit that needs no initial deposit from parents.

## Scheme change
MSF's page states that the SG Child Support Package, announced at the 2026 National Day Rally, will replace the Baby Bonus Scheme and Large Families Scheme. Children currently on the scheme will be transitioned automatically. Check the MSF page for the latest status and effective date.

## CDA
The CDA is a special co-savings account. Government support includes the CDA First Step Grant and dollar-for-dollar matching up to the applicable cap.

CDA funds can be used for approved healthcare and education expenses, including certain preschool and medical expenses.

The Child Savings Account (CSA) is automatically opened when the CDA is opened and is used to receive Baby Bonus Cash Gift and other government disbursements.

## Application routing
Baby Bonus Scheme applications and eligibility checks are made through LifeSG (life.gov.sg > Family & Parenting > Benefits & Support > Baby Bonus Scheme). MSF announced the transition of Baby Bonus applications to LifeSG from 1 July 2024.

## RAG response rule
If a user asks "Baby bonus got what?", explain the main components but quote the amounts above with the birth-order and date-of-birth conditions, and mention the SG Child Support Package transition.
