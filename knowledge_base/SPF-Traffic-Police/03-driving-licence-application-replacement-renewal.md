## Relevant tests and training
Depending on the class of licence, an applicant needs to pass the Basic Theory Test, Final Theory Test, Simulator Training and a practical driving or riding test. Motorcycle learners also pass the Riding Theory Test; Class 2B also requires an Expressway Familiarisation Ride. Class-specific requirements apply.

## Application
Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents can apply through SPF e-Services using Singpass.

Fees on the SPF page:
- New licence: S$50 (expected within 7 working days)
- Replacement of a lost, damaged or defaced licence: S$25 (no new photo; expected within 7 working days)
- Renewal for foreigners holding work passes: S$50, done at least one month before expiry (no reminder letters are sent). Renewal is rejected if the licence expired more than 3 years ago or the pass expires within one month.
- Adding a class: free endorsement sticker on the existing licence.

Aged 65 and above: a medical practitioner must certify fitness to drive; Classes 4, 4A and 5 renew at Singapore Safety Driving Centre.

## RAG response rule
Do not assume all driving licence classes have identical requirements. Retrieve the class-specific requirements when a user asks about a particular class.
