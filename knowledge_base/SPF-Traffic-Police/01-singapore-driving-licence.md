## Key information
A valid Singapore Driving Licence is required to drive or ride a motor vehicle on Singapore roads.

This applies to:
- Singapore Citizens
- Singapore Permanent Residents
- Foreigners residing in Singapore for more than 12 months
- Certain foreigners residing for less than 12 months who are employed as drivers and hold Work Permits or S Passes

Foreigners with valid foreign driving licences may need to convert their licence to a valid Singapore Driving Licence depending on their circumstances.

## Getting a Singapore driving licence
A learner can commence training at one of Singapore's three driving centres:
- Bukit Batok Driving Centre
- ComfortDelGro Driving Centre
- Singapore Safety Driving Centre

After passing the relevant theory and practical tests, applicants can apply for, replace or renew a Singapore Driving Licence through SPF e-Services, subject to the applicable rules.

## Validity and medical examination
- Classes 2B, 2A, 2, 3A, 3, 3C, 3CA: valid for 3 years if certified fit; medical re-examination every 3 years.
- Classes 4, 4P, 4A, 4AP, 5, 5P: valid for 1 year after passing the Proficiency Driving Test (PDT) or Enhanced PDT; yearly medical re-examination until age 74.
- Drivers aged 65 and above must be certified fit to drive at a registered clinic offering the Fitness To Drive assessment.

## Foreigners
- Residing less than 12 months: generally no conversion needed if holding a valid foreign licence plus an International Driving Permit or English translation (ASEAN nationals do not need an IDP).
- Conversion requires passing the Basic Theory Test and holding a valid foreign licence, with proof of residency in the issuing country (including at least six months of physical presence). Work pass holders get a Class 3C licence on conversion.
- From 15 September 2025, Class 3C holders must pass a road assessment before starting Class 4 lessons.

## Digital Driving Licence
A Digital Driving Licence is available in the Singpass app at no charge for those eligible for a Singpass account. The hardcopy photocard is not needed for most verification purposes.

## RAG response rule
For "How to get driving licence?", explain that the user needs to meet the relevant training and test requirements before applying for the licence.

For foreigners, retrieve the current conversion requirements instead of assuming that every foreign licence can be used indefinitely.
