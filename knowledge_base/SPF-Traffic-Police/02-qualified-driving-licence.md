# Traffic Police - Apply for Qualified Driving Licence

AGENCY: Singapore Police Force (SPF) / Traffic Police
CATEGORY: Driving
TOPIC: Qualified Driving Licence (QDL)
SOURCE_TYPE: Official Singapore Government Website
SOURCE_URL: https://www.police.gov.sg/E-Services/Apply-for-Qualified-Driving-Licence
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-28

## New application
After passing the practical test, an applicant must apply for the Qualified Driving Licence (QDL) online using Singpass.

For Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, a digital photograph is taken at the driving centre before the application.

Eligible foreigners who hold valid work passes from MOM or Student/Long-Term Visit passes from ICA and who passed their test at a Traffic Police Test Centre must also apply online using Singpass.

## Replacement
If a Singapore driving licence is lost or defaced, an eligible applicant can apply for a replacement online using Singpass.

If the original licence is subsequently found or was defaced, it must be returned to Traffic Police within 7 working days.

## Renewal
Foreigners with valid work passes from MOM or Student/Long-Term Visit passes from ICA can renew their licence online via Singpass when the licence is expiring in 1 month or less, subject to the applicable rules.

## Fees and timelines
- New licence: S$50
- Replacement: S$25 (inclusive of GST)
- Renewal: S$50
- Payment: credit/debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayNow
- The SPF e-service page states a processing lead time of 14 working days (the application page states 7 working days). Online renewal is not available more than 1 month before expiry, or to drivers aged 65 and above who must submit a medical report.

## Important
Driving or riding without a valid licence is a serious offence.

## RAG response rule
For "I lost my driving licence", route to the replacement process. For "my licence expiring", retrieve the renewal rules and check the user's status before giving a definitive answer.
