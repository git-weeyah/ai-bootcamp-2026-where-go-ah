## Purpose
Use this document to route questions about GST to IRAS.

## Current rate
IRAS states the current GST rate in Singapore is 9%.

## RAG routing
If a user asks:
- "What is GST?"
- "Why got GST?"
- "How does GST work?"
- "Do I need to register for GST?"
route the query to IRAS GST information.

GST registration and obligations depend on the applicable rules and business circumstances.

## RAG response rule
Do not infer that an individual needs GST registration simply because they earn income. GST registration is primarily a business/tax matter and the applicable threshold and conditions must be checked against the latest IRAS information.
