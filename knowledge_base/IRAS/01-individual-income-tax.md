## Who this covers
This topic covers individual income tax for employees, self-employed persons, sole proprietors, partnerships and other individuals, including Singapore Citizens, Singapore Permanent Residents and foreigners.

## Tax residency
Income tax rates depend on an individual's tax residency status.

IRAS states that a person may be treated as a tax resident for a Year of Assessment if they meet specified conditions, including conditions based on citizenship/residency or periods of stay/work in Singapore.

## Filing
Generally, an individual must submit an Income Tax Return if, in the preceding calendar year:
- total income was more than S$22,000; or
- self-employment net profit was more than S$6,000; or
- the person is a non-resident who derived income from Singapore.
