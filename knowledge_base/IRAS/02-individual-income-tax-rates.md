## Key information
Singapore's personal income tax rates depend on tax residency.

For tax resident individuals, Singapore uses progressive personal income tax rates.

For Year of Assessment (YA) 2024 onwards, resident rates:
| Chargeable income (S$) | Rate |
|---|---|
| First 20,000 | 0% |
| Next 10,000 | 2% |
| Next 10,000 | 3.5% |
| Next 40,000 | 7% |
| Next 40,000 | 11.5% |
| Next 40,000 | 15% |
| Next 40,000 | 18% |
| Next 40,000 | 19% |
| Next 40,000 | 19.5% |
| Next 40,000 | 20% |
| Next 180,000 | 22% |
| Next 500,000 | 23% |
| Above 1,000,000 | 24% |

Personal Income Tax Rebate: YA 2024 was 50% of tax payable capped at S$200; YA 2025 was 60% capped at S$200.

Non-resident individuals: employment income is taxed at the greater of a flat 15% or the progressive resident rates; director's fees, consultancy fees, rental, pension and other income are taxed at 24% from YA 2024.
